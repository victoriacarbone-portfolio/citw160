About This Guide

Urban Wildlife Coexistence Guide was created to provide clear, accessible information about living alongside wildlife in urban and suburban environments. Wildlife encounters can be confusing or stressful, especially when misinformation is widespread. This guide focuses on education, prevention, and humane solutions that prioritize both human safety and animal welfare.

Our Mission

The goal of this site is to encourage thoughtful, informed responses to wildlife encounters. By understanding why animals behave the way they do and how our actions influence them, communities can reduce unnecessary harm and avoid ineffective or inhumane practices.

What This Site Is and Isn't

This site is:

  • Educational and informational
  • Focused on humane, non-lethal solutions
  • Designed to promote coexistence and prevention

This site is not:

  • A wildlife removal service
  • A replacement for licensed rehabilitators
  • A source of emergency assistance

Student Project Disclaimer

This website was created as a student project for an HTML and CSS course and is intended for educational purposes only.
