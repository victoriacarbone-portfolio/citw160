Important Notice

If you encounter injured wildlife or an animal in immediate danger, please contact a licensed wildlife rehabilitator, local animal control, or your state wildlife agency. Do not attempt to handle wildlife without proper training.

Learning how to respond appropriately to wildlife encounters helps protect both people and animals. Thank you for taking the time to learn more about humane coexistence.
